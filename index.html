MLB hitters with 400+ pitches · 2026 regular season · Baseball Savant data

Total = Swing decisions + Contact + Power, each worth an equal share. Decisions = 2 × z Decision value. Contact = z Z-contact% + z Squared-up%. Power = ⅔ × (z EV90 + z Hard-hit% + z Sweet spot%).
Decision value credits each swing or take with the league-average run value of that choice, for that pitch location and count, compared with the typical choice there (runs per 100 pitches; 0 = average). It uses league outcomes only, so a hitter's own contact and power don't leak in. z-scores are versus hitters with 1,000+ pitches (n=310); in that group the Total has an SD of about 2.8.
