2026 Hitter Score

MLB hitters with 400+ pitches · 2026 regular season · Baseball Savant data

Each area is an index in standard deviations above or below average (versus hitters with 1,000+ pitches, n=310): Decisions = z decision value; Contact = average of z Z-contact% and z squared-up%; Power = average of z EV90 and z barrels per batted ball.
Total (1-2-3) = 1×Decisions + 2×Contact + 3×Power. These weights come from regressing wRC+ on the three areas across 2024–2026 (942 hitter-seasons); the fitted weights were about 0.7 : 2.0 : 3.3 for the same season and 0.6 : 2.0 : 3.4 for predicting the next one. Equal-wt total = 2×each area, a skills profile for spotting hitters who are elite in one or two areas. Custom uses your weights. SD among regulars: Total 2.6, equal-weight 2.7.
Decision value credits each swing or take with the league-average run value of that choice for that pitch location and count, compared with the typical choice there (runs per 100 pitches; 0 = average). Pool means (SD): decision value +0.02 (0.34), Z-contact 84.3% (5.1), squared-up 32.6% (4.4), EV90 104.2 (2.7), barrels per batted ball 8.1% (4.0).
