BDL 2027 Pitcher Projections

Steamer ERA adjusted for Stuff+ and projected team defense · 834 pitchers on FanGraphs Depth Charts

BDL ERA starts from Steamer’s 2027 ERA and adds three adjustments: Stuff (about −0.03 ERA per point of regressed Stuff+ above average; Location+ adds almost nothing), Avg (Steamer’s distance from league average is cut by 26%, which only helps once Stuff+ is in), and Def (−0.8 ERA per run per 9 innings of projected team defense: each fielder’s regressed 2024–26 DRS times his Depth Charts playing time; a +30-run defense is worth about −0.15 ERA). Free agents get league-average defense until they sign. The average projected ERA is unchanged from Steamer’s.
Backtest. Each season from 2021 to 2026 was projected with weights fit on the other seasons and graded on every pitcher with a preseason Steamer projection and 30+ MLB innings (2,812 pitcher-seasons; IP-weighted ERA error, both systems shifted to each season’s average). Steamer 1.065, BDL 1.055, better in 100% of bootstrap resamples; using only earlier seasons to fit, 1.057 → 1.051. For pitchers with 60+ IP, the Stuff and Avg adjustments alone cut the error from 0.943 to 0.931. Single-season ERA is mostly noise, so these are small gains: Steamer explains about 17% of the spread in pitcher ERA and BDL about 18%. Steamer does not account for team defense; DRS beat Statcast OAA and Fielding Run Value as the defense measure, and Pitching+, PitchingBot, and plain stats (K−BB%, SIERA, xFIP) added less than Stuff+.
Caveats. FanGraphs computes Stuff+ for past seasons with its current model, which may flatter the backtest a little. Extreme Stuff+ relievers (sidearmers especially) get large adjustments; treat those with some skepticism. Pitchers with no MLB pitch data get essentially no Stuff adjustment.
