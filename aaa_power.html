2026 AAA Power

Quality-of-contact detail · Triple-A hitters with 400+ pitches · 2026 regular season

Power index = average of z EV90 and z barrels per batted ball, each within the hitter's league (same formula as MLB; a barrel needs both high exit velocity and a good launch angle, so hard ground balls don't count). Hard-hit% and sweet spot% are reference only. SLG, ISO, xSLG and xISO are not park-adjusted, and the PCL's thinner air in places like Albuquerque, Las Vegas, Reno and Salt Lake inflates them; exit velocity and launch angle are much less affected. Bat speed and blasts aren't published for the minors.
