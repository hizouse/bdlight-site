Bat-to-ball detail · MLB hitters with 400+ pitches · 2026 regular season

Contact score = z Z-contact% + z Squared-up%, the same as on the overview. Z-contact% is how often he makes contact on swings at strikes (1 − whiffs ÷ swings; foul tips count as contact). Squared-up% is how precisely he makes contact: the share of contact (including fouls) that reached at least 80% of the maximum exit velocity possible for his bat speed and the pitch speed (Statcast bat tracking). Squared-up% is nearly unrelated to how hard a hitter hits the ball, so it isn't power in disguise; across 2024–2026 it added value beyond Z-contact and power.
O-contact% and overall contact% are for reference and are not in the score.
Pool means (SD), 1,000+ pitches: Z-contact 84.3% (5.1), squared-up 32.6% (4.4), O-contact 57.3% (10.5), contact% 75.4% (6.4).
