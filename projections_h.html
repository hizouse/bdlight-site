BDL 2027 Hitter Projections

Steamer600, BDL, and their 50/50 blend · 574 hitters

Blend is the average of Steamer600 and BDL. Pitcher ERA projections are on the BDL P Projections page.
BDL projects next-season OBP and SLG from three years of FanGraphs results and Baseball Savant data (expected stats, exit velocity and barrels, swing decisions and contact, sprint speed), each regressed toward league average by sample size, plus minor-league lines for hitters with little MLB time (adjusted for minor-league park factors), age, and the home park. BDL figures are set to Steamer’s league level so the two are comparable.
Backtest. Each season from 2019 to 2026 (2020 skipped) was projected using only earlier seasons and graded on hitters with 400+ PA who stayed with their projected team (1,328 hitter-seasons; PA-weighted error in points, projections shifted to the sample average). OPS: BDL 70.0, Steamer 71.9, ZiPS 71.9, blend 69.6. SLG: BDL 50.2, Steamer 52.1, blend 50.2. OBP: BDL 25.5, Steamer 25.7, blend 25.1. BDL’s edge is in SLG; on OBP it is about even with Steamer, and Steamer was slightly better in 2026 alone. BDL does not predict the league run level better than Steamer, which is why it borrows Steamer’s.
wOBA and xwOBA for 2026 and 2025 are shown for reference. BDL replaces the earlier luck, contact, and young-xwOBA adjustments to Steamer.
