2026 Power

Quality-of-contact detail · MLB hitters with 400+ pitches · 2026 regular season

Power index = average of z EV90 and z barrels per batted ball, as on the overview. EV90 measures raw strength and is very stable year to year; a barrel needs high exit velocity and a good launch angle, so hard ground balls don't count. In 2024–2026 tests this pair predicted extra bases per batted ball and next-season wRC+ better than the earlier EV90 / hard-hit% / sweet spot% version, and sweet spot added nothing once barrels were in. Hard-hit% and sweet spot% stay here for reference, along with xISO = xSLG − xBA, Statcast's batted-ball types, and bat speed and blast% from bat tracking.
