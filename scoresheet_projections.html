Scoresheet 2027 Adjusted Projections

Steamer600 rate stats adjusted for 2026 luck, contact, and young-hitter xwOBA · 574 hitters on the Rankings tab

Adjusted 2027 projections start from Steamer600 OBP and SLG and add three corrections for patterns Steamer missed in every season from 2023 to 2026 (690 hitter-seasons, 400+ PA, same team as projected). Each uses the hitter's 2026 numbers, in standard deviations (z), and the fitted points per SD:
Luck (2026 wOBA − xwOBA): −1.5 OBP, −5.8 SLG per SD. Hitters who beat their xwOBA were overprojected the next year, and hitters below it underprojected (one SD ≈ 20 points of wOBA).
Contact (the Contact index from the Contact page): −3.2 OBP, −7.0 SLG per SD. Steamer overprojected high-contact hitters and underprojected low-contact sluggers; this was driven by Z-contact%, not squared-up%, and was not explained by power, luck, or age.
Young xwOBA (hitters 26 or younger in 2027): −2.9 OBP, −13.1 SLG per SD of 2026 xwOBA. Steamer already builds in growth for young hitters with strong contact quality, and then some.
Hitters with fewer than 100 PA in 2026 get no luck or young-xwOBA adjustment, and hitters with fewer than 400 pitches get no Contact adjustment, so rookies keep their Steamer line. Tested out of sample (fit on 2023–25, applied to 2026), the three adjustments cut Steamer's OPS error from 70.1 to 68.8 points and beat plain Steamer in 94% of bootstrap resamples. That is about as much as averaging Steamer with ZiPS gained. The coefficients shown are refit on all four seasons. Decisions stats, hard-hit%, sweet-spot%, bat speed, blast%, EV90, and barrels added nothing beyond these. The adjusted OBP, SLG, and OPS feed the 2027 Rankings.
