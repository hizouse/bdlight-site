FT Poach Draft: Prospects

Top prospects by Baseball America, FanGraphs, and bdlight.net’s own Prospect Ranks, with BL Final Table teams and protections · 69 players

FT poach draft. Teams are BL Final Table rosters as of Nov 12 (Scoresheet’s page) with two trades it hadn’t processed yet: Cal Raleigh to Team 15 for Kyle Teel, Seiya Suzuki, and Victor Mesa Jr., and Oneil Cruz to Team 24 for Sandy Alcantara and Tyler Glasnow. Protections are the final lists (5 per team), except that Team 15 acquired protected Francisco Lindor from Team 12 on Nov 15 (after the deadline, only protected players can be traded), so Team 15 has 6 protected players and Team 12 has 4. Gray = protected; green = Team 18 (darker green = Team 18 protected). Available = on an FT roster and not protected (and not yet drafted). Age filters use 2027 season age. Drafted players (the draft starts Dec 1) will be marked with their new team and round as picks come in.
Who is listed. Anyone in the top 50 of Baseball America’s latest Top 100, the top 25 of FanGraphs’ 2026 pre-trade-deadline Top 100, or the top 25 of any of the four lists on bdlight.net’s Prospect Ranks (10+ career WAR, a 3+ WAR season, 20+ career WAR, a 5+ WAR season); nobody else. Each player’s rank in all six lists is shown when he is in them (BA ranks recorded through 50; FanGraphs through 100; the BDL lists cover hitters only, so pitchers have no BDL ranks). Rk follows Baseball America’s order; players BA doesn’t rank in its top 50 follow, by best rank in any list. BDL comp averages a hitter’s four BDL ranks. Combined = (2 × BA + FG + BDL comp) / 4, so BA counts double; a player BA doesn’t rank counts as 51 and one FanGraphs doesn’t rank as 101, and pitchers (no BDL ranks) use (2 × BA + FG) / 3. Click any header to re-sort, for example by Combined. Choose Show: Available for the prospects you can poach; players not on an FT roster are listed but can’t be poached. Level and ETA are from the FanGraphs prospect board.
