2026 AAA Hitter Score

Triple-A hitters with 400+ pitches · 2026 regular season · Baseball Savant minor-league Statcast data

Same three areas as the MLB score, but every z-score compares a hitter only with his own league: the International League or the Pacific Coast League (1,000+ pitches; a hitter who played in both is assigned to the league where he saw more pitches). Decisions = z decision value. Contact = average of z Z-contact% and z hard-hit balls per heart swing, which stands in for squared-up% because bat tracking isn't published for the minors. Power = average of z EV90 and z barrels per batted ball, as on the MLB pages.
Age factor = how many SDs younger than his league's regulars he is (age on June 30, 2026; regulars average 26.4 in the IL and 27.4 in the PCL, SD about 2.6 years).
Total = 1×Decisions + 2×Contact + 3×Power + 2×Age factor. Skills total is the MLB 1-2-3 formula without age; Equal-wt total = 2×each skill area; Custom uses your weights (starts at the Total weights). No park adjustments. wOBA is shown for reference only. SD among regulars: Total 4.0, skills total 3.2, equal-weight 2.9.
Hard-hit per heart swing is partly a power measure: among regulars it correlates 0.73 with hard-hit% and 0.63 with EV90, versus 0.14 with Z-contact%.
