2026 AAA Contact

Bat-to-ball detail · Triple-A hitters with 400+ pitches · 2026 regular season

Contact index = average of z Z-contact% and z hard-hit balls per heart swing, each within the hitter's league. Z-contact% is how often he makes contact on swings at strikes. HH per heart swing is how often a swing at a middle-of-the-zone pitch (Statcast's heart region, all counts) ends as a ball hit 95+ mph. It replaces squared-up%, which isn't available in the minors; it rewards both making contact and hitting the ball hard, so it overlaps with power (r = 0.73 with hard-hit%).
O-contact% and contact% are reference only.
