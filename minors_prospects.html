2026 Prospect Ranks

Chance of MLB success from 2026 Low-A through Triple-A stats

The chance each 2026 Low-A, High-A, AA, and AAA hitter (150+ PA at a level, under 130 career MLB at-bats through 2026) becomes a 10+ career WAR player, has a 3+ WAR season, reaches 20+ career WAR, or has a 5+ WAR season. A logistic regression trained on 2007–2014 seasons (career outcomes) and 2007–2018 seasons (single-season outcomes), then recalibrated; outcomes are total FanGraphs position-player WAR (fWAR: batting, baserunning, fielding, positional). Inputs, each compared with the player's own league that season: age vs. league (birthdate, July 1), park-adjusted wOBA, K%, ISO, BB%, plus Speed Score, SwStr%, Pull%, and GB/LD/FB rates; the prior season's age and stats; level; prior MLB at-bats; and share of games at each position. A player at two levels gets a PA-weighted blend.
Biggest factors for 10+ WAR (odds multiplier, all else equal): each year younger than league ×2.4; +1 SD wOBA ×1.9; +1 SD ISO ×1.4; +1 SD Speed ×1.3; 1 SD fewer strikeouts ×1.35; a full season at C or SS ×5.5, CF ×4 (vs. 1B/DH).
Cross-validated accuracy (AUC): 10+ WAR .89, 3+ WAR season .89, 20+ WAR .92, 5+ WAR season .93 (AAA seasons alone: .88, .87, .90, .92). Caveat: a player who DHed because of an injury is treated like a 1B/DH. Hitters with 130+ career MLB at-bats through 2026 aren't included.
