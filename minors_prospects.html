2026 Prospect Ranks

Chance of MLB success from 2026 Low-A, High-A, and Double-A stats

The chance each 2026 Low-A, High-A, and AA hitter (150+ PA at a level) becomes a 10+ career WAR player, has a 3+ WAR season, reaches 20+ career WAR, or has a 4+ WAR season. A logistic regression trained on 2007–2014 seasons (career outcomes) and 2007–2018 seasons (single-season outcomes), then recalibrated; outcomes are FanGraphs MLB batting WAR. Inputs, each compared with the player's own league that season: age vs. league (birthdate, July 1), park-adjusted wOBA, K%, ISO, BB%, plus Speed Score, SwStr%, Pull%, and GB/LD/FB rates; the prior season's age and stats; level; and share of games at each position. A player at two levels gets a PA-weighted blend.
Biggest factors for 10+ WAR (odds multiplier, all else equal): each year younger than league ×2.4; +1 SD wOBA ×1.9; +1 SD Speed ×1.3; +1 SD ISO ×1.3; 1 SD fewer strikeouts ×1.5; a full season at C or SS ×5, CF ×3.3, 3B ×1.6 (vs. 1B/DH).
Cross-validated accuracy (AUC): 10+ WAR .89, 3+ WAR season .89, 20+ WAR .91, 4+ WAR season .91. Base rates in training: 4.2%, 4.9%, 1.8%, 2.8%. Caveat: a player who DHed because of an injury is treated like a 1B/DH. Hitters who spent most of 2026 in AAA or MLB aren't included.
Hover over a column header for its definition. Click a header to sort; click again to reverse. Position and level filters match any listed position or level. Data: FanGraphs minor- and major-league leaderboards; birthdates from the Chadwick Bureau register; games by position from the MLB Stats API; park factors built from MLB Stats API home/road splits.
