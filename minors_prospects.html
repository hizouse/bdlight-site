2026 Prospect Ranks

Chance of MLB success from 2026 Low-A through Triple-A stats

The chance each 2026 Low-A, High-A, AA, and AAA hitter (under 130 career MLB at-bats through 2026) becomes a 10+ career WAR player, has a 3+ WAR season, reaches 20+ career WAR, or has a 5+ WAR season.
Level
How it works: a logistic regression trained on minor-league seasons at Low-A and above (2007–2014 seasons for career outcomes, 2007–2018 for single-season outcomes). Its raw probabilities are then recalibrated: the model is refit five times, each time leaving out a fifth of the players and predicting them, and the predictions for those unseen players are compared with what actually happened to them. A small correction curve is fit so that, for example, players given 20% really did succeed about 20% of the time. In practice it pulls the most extreme probabilities slightly toward the middle. Outcomes are total FanGraphs position-player WAR (fWAR: batting, baserunning, fielding, positional). The model uses up to two years of minor-league stats (the current season and the prior one); every stat is compared with the player's league that season, and K%, BB%, ISO, and wOBA are also park-adjusted. A player at two levels gets a PA-weighted blend.
Major factors (odds multiplier for 10+ WAR, all else equal): age vs. league (birthdate, July 1), ×2.4 per year younger; park-adjusted wOBA, ×1.9 per SD; park-adjusted ISO, ×1.4 per SD; Speed Score, ×1.35 per SD; park-adjusted K%, ×1.35 per SD fewer strikeouts; share of games at each position, for a full season at C or SS ×5.5, CF ×4, 3B ×1.9, 2B ×1.1 (vs. 1B/DH; LF/RF ≈ 1B/DH).
Minor factors: park-adjusted BB%; SwStr%, Pull%, and GB/LD/FB rates; level; and prior MLB at-bats. The prior season's age and stats are included but weighted differently from the current season's.
Cross-validated accuracy (AUC): 10+ WAR .89, 3+ WAR season .89, 20+ WAR .92, 5+ WAR season .93 (AAA seasons alone: .88, .87, .90, .92). Caveat: a player who DHed because of an injury is treated like a 1B/DH.
Who's covered: hitters with 150+ PA in 2026 at Low-A or higher and fewer than 130 career MLB at-bats through 2026. A hitter with fewer than 150 such PA in 2026 (usually because of injury) is included if he had 150+ PA at a level in 2025 (Stats = “2025”). His probability starts from his 2025 season, is lowered to reflect how hitters who missed most of a season have historically fared, and is adjusted for how he hit in his limited 2026 PA; it never ends up higher than his 2025 season alone would give. Arizona Fall League and other off-season stats are not used.
