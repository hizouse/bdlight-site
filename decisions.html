Decisions score = z Heart swing% (adj.) + z Chase swing% (flipped), the same as on the overview. Heart = middle of the zone (Savant attack zones 1–9); chase = just off the edges (attack zones 21–29). Adj. heart swing% leaves out 3-0 pitches and off-speed/breaking pitches in 0-0, 1-0 and 2-0 counts.
Z-swing% and O-swing% use the rulebook strike zone, like FanGraphs. They are shown for reference and are not in the score.
