2026 Swing Decisions

Plate discipline detail · MLB hitters with 400+ pitches · 2026 regular season

Decisions index = z decision value, as on the overview. Decision value: every 2026 pitch was binned by location (mirrored for lefties) and count; for each bin, the league-average run value of swinging vs. taking was computed. Each swing or take earns the league value of that choice minus the typical choice in that bin, in runs per 100 pitches (0 = average). The swing and take parts split that value by the hitter's choice, shown relative to average.
Reference only: heart/chase swing rates (Statcast attack regions), rulebook Z-swing% and O-swing%, d-prime (eye: swinging at strikes more than balls) and swing tendency (how much he swings overall).
Why decision value: across 2024–2026 it added the most wRC+/wOBA beyond contact and power of any decision measure tested, held up in all three seasons, is stable year to year, and drives walk rate strongly.
Pool means (SD), 1,000+ pitches: decision value +0.02 (0.34), heart swing% (adj.) 77.0%, chase 25.8%, Z-swing 66.7%, O-swing 30.0%, d-prime 0.97 (0.16), swing tendency 0.05 (0.14).
