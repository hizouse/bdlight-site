Scoresheet 2027 Defense Projections

Projected range and errors for 525 fielder lines, and SB/CS ratings for 122 catchers · hitters on the Rankings tab · jump to catchers

Range. The projected 2027 Scoresheet range comes from 2025–26 DRS and OAA with errors backed out, lowered for heavy DH use at OF and 1B, and held within ±.07 of the 2026 range, as Scoresheet did for 2026. Players get one line per position where they played 20+ games in 2026. Highlighted OF ranges of 2.16+ are above the ~2.15 average center fielder, whose range counts about 1.4× in the sim.
Errors. Projected from each player’s MLB errors per chance over recent seasons, weighted toward the latest and regressed heavily toward the position average, then converted to errors per game at the position’s average chances. Err adj is in range units: one extra error per 10 games = −0.07 range.
Def adj is the total in OPS points, as added on the 2027 Rankings at OBP weight 1.0: range minus the position average plus the error adjustment, at 0.10 range = .027 OBP + .027 SLG.
Catchers have no Scoresheet range. Their projected SB and CS ratings are valued at SB +0.20 and CS −0.42 runs, scaled to the sim’s lower steal volume (70% of MLB), plus a catcher error adjustment.
