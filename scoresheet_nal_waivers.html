Who is listed. For each of the 12 teams, the 12 best players by regular Steamer 2027 projection (with playing time): RA9-WAR for pitchers, WAR for hitters. Each team protects 10 in the waiver draft, so the bold names are the top 10 by this measure and the two plain names are the likeliest to be exposed.
Exempt players are left off: rookies (an “m” before the player number on the current roster page) and players who lost rookie eligibility in 2026 (an “m” on the 2025 roster page).
Top reliever. Each team’s best reliever by RA9-WAR (under 5 projected starts) is marked RP. Only Jhoan Duran (Team 2) is already in his team’s top 12; the other relievers are added in purple below the pitchers and are not counted in the 12.
Steamer600 block. Below the hitters (peach): players who would be in the team’s top 12 if everyone were projected at full playing time (Steamer600: 600 PA, 200 IP for starters) but are not already listed—mostly injured or part-time players such as Schwellenbach, Hunter Greene, Ben Brown, and Strider. Each shows the Steamer600 value and its rank on the team, the regular value, and regular Steamer playing time.
Pitchers are listed first and hitters start on the same row for every team; each group is sorted by projection. Ages are as of Nov 11, 2026. Ohtani is split into a pitcher and a hitter on the Scoresheet roster but counts as one player, so he is listed once, with the pitchers (yellow), at his combined value (2.2 pitching RA9-WAR + 4.2 hitting WAR = 6.3).
