OBP/SLG/OPS are a 50/50 blend of Steamer600 and BDL projections. Defensive ranges and errors are projected by Claude. See below for more details.

OBP weight
Adj OPS = blend OPS (the average of Steamer600 and BDL on the Projections tab) plus a defense adjustment and a stolen-base adjustment, both in OPS terms. Non-catchers: the projected 2027 Scoresheet range comes from 2025–26 DRS and OAA with errors backed out, lowered for heavy DH use at OF and 1B, and held within ±.07 of the 2026 range, as Scoresheet did for 2026. The defense adjustment is that range minus the position average, at 0.10 range = .027 OBP + .027 SLG, the same for infielders and outfielders (measured from 2,700 team-seasons of 20+ team BLs, 2022–26: 0.10 range prevents about 0.10 hits and 0.07 runs per game), plus an error adjustment (one extra error per 10 games = −0.07 range; each error costs about 0.5 runs in those leagues, nearly all of it unearned). Errors are projected from each player's MLB errors per chance over recent seasons, weighted toward the latest, and regressed heavily toward the position average (single seasons of errors are mostly luck, so only long track records move far from average); the rate is then converted to errors per game at the position's average chances per game, so rangier fielders are not penalized for reaching more balls. All outfielders are valued alike; highlighted OF ranges of 2.16+ are above the ~2.15 average center fielder, whose range counts about 1.4× in the sim. Catchers: projected SB/CS ratings valued with run values measured from Scoresheet's own sim (SB +0.20, CS −0.42 runs) and scaled to the sim's lower steal volume (70% of MLB), plus a catcher error adjustment. DH lines carry no defense value. Stolen bases: Steamer SB and CS vs an average runner at the same run values, floored at zero on the assumption that runners below the ~68% break-even are toggled off (N).
VORP 20 is the player's margin over a rough replacement level: the Adj OPS of the 21st-ranked line at each position (61st for OF). DH lines use the 1B replacement levels. Players eligible at several positions (20+ games there in 2026) appear once per position and count toward each position's ranking. Hitters with no 20-game position in 2026 (mostly prospects and injured players) are listed at their Scoresheet or Steamer position.
OBP weight. At 1.0 the base is OPS. At 1.4 it is mOPS = 1.4 × OBP + SLG, the weighting measured from 2,700 team-seasons of 20+ team BLs (2022–26); range and error adjustments (non-catchers) are multiplied by 1.189, because the hits that range and errors add or prevent are singles, and a single moves OBP and SLG almost equally (by 1/PA and 1/AB, a ratio of about 0.90), so on the mOPS scale it counts 1.189 times as much as on the OPS scale, the same as a single from the bat; the stolen-base and catcher SB/CS adjustments, which are not hits, are multiplied by 1.134, the ratio of runs per point of OPS to runs per point of mOPS in the same data, so they keep their run value. Stolen-base and catcher values use SB +0.20 and CS −0.42 runs, measured in those same leagues with OBP and SLG held constant.
League. MLB ranks everyone with VORP 20 (21st-ranked line at each position, 61st at OF, for a 24-team BL). AL or NL shows that league’s players plus free agents and uses VORP 10 (11th, or 31st at OF), with replacement levels computed from that league’s players only.
