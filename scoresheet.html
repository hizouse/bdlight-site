Scoresheet 2027 Hitter Rankings

Hitters projected for 1.0+ WAR by Steamer600 · one line per eligible position · 650 lines

Adj OPS = Steamer600 OPS plus a defense adjustment and a stolen-base adjustment, both in OPS terms. Non-catchers: projected 2027 Scoresheet range (lowered for heavy DH use at OF and 1B) minus the position average, at 0.10 range = .032 OBP + .032 SLG for infielders and .027 + .027 for outfielders (measured from Scoresheet team results), plus an error adjustment (one extra error per 10 games = −0.10 range, at the infield rate). All outfielders are valued alike; highlighted OF ranges of 2.16+ are above the ~2.15 average center fielder, whose range counts about 1.4× in the sim. Catchers: projected SB/CS ratings valued with run values measured from Scoresheet's own sim (SB +0.14, CS −0.41 runs) and scaled to the sim's lower steal volume (70% of MLB), plus a catcher error adjustment. DH lines carry no defense value. Stolen bases: Steamer SB and CS vs an average runner at the same run values, floored at zero on the assumption that runners below the ~75% break-even are toggled off (N). Platoon splits are not included.
Rep 1 / Rep 2 are rough replacement levels: the Adj OPS of the 11th- and 21st-ranked lines at each position (31st and 61st for OF), and VORP 10 / VORP 20 are the player's margins over each. DH lines use the 1B replacement levels (there are too few DH lines to set their own). Players eligible at several positions (20+ games there in 2026) appear once per position and count toward each position's ranking. Hitters with no 20-game position in 2026 (mostly prospects and injured players) are listed at their Scoresheet or Steamer position.
