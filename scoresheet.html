Adj OPS = adjusted Steamer600 OPS (Steamer600 with the luck, contact, and young-xwOBA adjustments on the Projections tab) plus a defense adjustment and a stolen-base adjustment, both in OPS terms. Non-catchers: projected 2027 Scoresheet range (from 2025–26 DRS and OAA with errors backed out, lowered for heavy DH use at OF and 1B, and held within ±.07 of the 2026 range, as Scoresheet did for 2026) minus the position average, at 0.10 range = .031 OBP + .031 SLG (measured from 2024–26 Scoresheet team results), plus an error adjustment (one extra error per 10 games = −0.10 range). All outfielders are valued alike; highlighted OF ranges of 2.16+ are above the ~2.15 average center fielder, whose range counts about 1.4× in the sim. Catchers: projected SB/CS ratings valued with run values measured from Scoresheet's own sim (SB +0.20, CS −0.42 runs) and scaled to the sim's lower steal volume (70% of MLB), plus a catcher error adjustment. DH lines carry no defense value. Stolen bases: Steamer SB and CS vs an average runner at the same run values, floored at zero on the assumption that runners below the ~68% break-even are toggled off (N). Platoon splits are not included.
VORP 20 is the player's margin over a rough replacement level: the Adj OPS of the 21st-ranked line at each position (61st for OF). DH lines use the 1B replacement levels (there are too few DH lines to set their own). Players eligible at several positions (20+ games there in 2026) appear once per position and count toward each position's ranking. Hitters with no 20-game position in 2026 (mostly prospects and injured players) are listed at their Scoresheet or Steamer position.
OBP weight. At 1.0 the base is OPS. At 1.4 it is mOPS = 1.4 × OBP + SLG, the weighting measured from 2,700 team-seasons of 20+ team BLs (2022–26); the stolen-base, range, error, and catcher adjustments are then multiplied by 1.134, the ratio of runs per point of OPS to runs per point of mOPS in the same data, so each adjustment is worth the same number of runs on either scale. Stolen-base and catcher values use SB +0.20 and CS −0.42 runs, measured in those same leagues with OBP and SLG held constant.
