Non-catchers: projected 2027 Scoresheet range plus an error adjustment, minus the position average, at 0.10 range = .025 OBP + .025 SLG. Rep 1 / Rep 2 are rough replacement levels: the Adj OPS of the 11th- and 21st-ranked lines at each position (31st and 61st for OF), and vs Rep is the player's margin over each. Players eligible at several positions (20+ games there in 2026) appear once per position and count toward each position's ranking.
